# Code of Conduct

ODE Trail follows the Open Data Ensemble Code of Conduct, which applies across every
repository, discussion, and event in the organisation.

**→ [Read it here](https://github.com/OpenDataEnsemble/.github/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)**

The short version: be kind, assume good faith, and take responsibility when something goes
wrong. For many people Community Day is their first experience of open source — make it a
good one.

## Reporting a problem

Email <hello@opendataensemble.org>, or contact staff privately through the
[forum](https://forum.opendataensemble.org). At Community Day you can also speak to any
host on the day. Reports are handled discreetly.
